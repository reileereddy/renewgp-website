Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Who we are

RenewGP ("we", "us", "our", "the Company") is a vehicle licensing and registration services business based at Shop 4, 11 Finch St, Atlasville, Boksburg, 1459, South Africa. We can be contacted at renewgp.info@gmail.com, +27 64 034 8539, or +27 74 297 2420.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website (renewgp.co.za) and to the services we provide to you in person, telephonically, via WhatsApp, or by email. By using our services or this website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use our services or continue browsing this website.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

  • anyone who visits our website; and
  • anyone who engages us as a customer to carry out a vehicle licensing, registration, or related service.

3. Definitions

  • Personal Information means information relating to an identifiable, living natural person, or an identifiable existing juristic person (such as a company), as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).
  • Processing means anything done with personal information — including collecting, recording, storing, using, sharing, or destroying it — as defined in POPIA.
  • You or the customer means any person who uses our services or accesses our website, including someone acting on behalf of another person or an organisation.
  • Service Provider means any third party we may use to help deliver our services (for example, a courier, or the relevant government licensing authority).
  • Website means renewgp.co.za.

4. Legal capacity and minors

Our services are intended for use by adults (18 years or older) or by a parent/legal guardian acting on behalf of a minor. Some of our services — such as ID and learner's licence photos — are commonly used by minors applying for a learner's licence. Where a minor's personal information is provided to us, we will treat this as having been provided with the knowledge and consent of that minor's parent or legal guardian, and it is the responsibility of the accompanying adult to ensure they are authorised to provide that consent.

5. Your obligations when sharing information with us

You may only give us personal information about yourself, or about another person where you have that person's permission to do so (for example, submitting documents on behalf of a family member or an employer). By providing us with someone else's personal information, you confirm that you are authorised to share it with us for the purpose of the service being requested.

6. Information we collect

Depending on the service you request, we may collect:

  • Identifying information — your full name, ID number, date of birth, or Traffic Register Number
  • Contact information — your phone number, email address, or WhatsApp number
  • Address information — your physical or postal address
  • Vehicle and document information — vehicle registration numbers, VIN/chassis numbers, NaTIS records, and copies of related certificates or forms
  • Payment reference information — where fees are paid via EFT or bank transfer, we may see the proof of payment or reference details associated with that transaction. We do not store full banking card details, and we do not process online card payments through this website.

We collect this information directly from you — in person, via WhatsApp, phone, or email — when you request a service from us. We do not currently collect personal information automatically through this website (there is no account registration, contact form, or tracking on this site).

7. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • provide the specific service you've requested, including preparing and submitting applications, renewals, or notifications to the relevant licensing authority (such as the RTMC or Gauteng provincial licensing departments) on your behalf;
  • contact you regarding the progress, completion, or any issue with your request;
  • keep the records necessary to meet our own legal and administrative obligations; and
  • improve how we run our services.

We do not use your personal information for direct marketing, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it to third parties.

8. How we process your information

In line with POPIA, we commit to the following when processing your personal information:

  • We only collect personal information for a purpose connected to the service you've asked us to perform.
  • We only process personal information that is adequate and relevant for that purpose — we don't ask for more than we need.
  • We will not use your personal information for a different, unrelated purpose without your consent, unless required or permitted by law.
  • We keep records of what personal information was collected and why, for as long as reasonably necessary.
  • We securely destroy or delete personal information once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for, or once our legal recordkeeping obligations have been met.

9. Who we share your information with

We only share your personal information in the following situations:

  • With licensing authorities: to complete your request, we share the necessary information with the relevant government department or authority, such as the RTMC or Gauteng provincial licensing offices.
  • With service providers: where we use a third party to help deliver part of a service (for example, a courier), we only share what's necessary for them to carry out that task.
  • For legal reasons: if required to do so by law, or in response to a valid request from a court, regulator, or government authority.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose, only with your explicit permission.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

10. How long we keep your information

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to complete your request and to meet any legal or recordkeeping obligations that apply to our business, after which it is securely destroyed or deleted.

11. Security of your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational precautions to keep your documents and information secure, including limiting who has access to physical and digital copies of your documents, and disposing of documents securely once they are no longer needed. However, no method of storage or transmission (including over the internet, WhatsApp, or email) is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information sent to us through these channels.

12. Cookies and website tracking

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking technology of any kind. If this changes in future — for example, if we add website analytics — this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be displayed on the site to obtain your consent where required.

13. Links to other websites

Our website may occasionally link to third-party websites or services. We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, the privacy practices of any third-party website. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

14. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • ask us what personal information we hold about you;
  • request that we correct inaccurate information;
  • request that we delete your information, subject to any legal retention requirements;
  • object to how your information is being used; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at renewgp.info@gmail.com.

15. Information Officer

Any queries regarding this policy or your personal information can be directed to our Information Officer via renewgp.info@gmail.com.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example due to legal changes or changes in how we operate. Continuing to use our services or website after a revised policy is posted means you agree to the updated version. The current version was last updated on the date shown below.
