Standard deregistration / scrapping

R572
What to bring
  • Police affidavit stating why the vehicle must be deregistered or scrapped, describing the vehicle's registration number, licence number, and chassis number
  • Original green Natis (RC1) / vehicle registration document
  • Copy of the owner's ID document
  • Photos of the scrapped vehicle
  • Proof of address (utility bill or first page of a bank statement) — if the utility bill is not in your name, attach an affidavit from the account holder confirming you live there, plus a copy of their ID; if you live in an informal settlement, bring an officially date-stamped letter from your ward councillor confirming your address instead

Export — buyer unknown

R572

A vehicle can't be deregistered if a new owner never completed the change of ownership. If that new owner has disappeared or can't be traced, an "export — buyer unknown" application is required instead.

What to bring
  • Police affidavit confirming the vehicle was sold — including the sale date and amount, and explaining that the new owner can't be located to complete the change of ownership. It must also describe the vehicle's registration number, licence number, and chassis number
  • Original green Natis (RC1)
  • Copy of the current owner's ID document (the person the vehicle was sold to)

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to deregister a scrapped vehicle?

If a vehicle is no longer in working condition — or you'd like to use it purely as, say, a garden feature — it needs to be formally deregistered rather than just left off the road.

What if the new owner never changed the vehicle into their name?

A vehicle can't be deregistered if a new owner failed to do the change of ownership. If that owner can't be traced, an "export — buyer unknown" application is required instead.

Do outstanding fees and fines matter?

Yes. Outstanding licence fees and any fines on the vehicle must be fully settled up to the date of deregistration before we can proceed.

How much does deregistration cost?

R572 (government and RenewGP fees combined), and it takes about 4 days.
