Before you go

Documents you will need

Pick the option that matches you.

Individuals (natural persons)

What to bring
  • A valid foreign passport
  • A valid visa from Home Affairs
  • An original certified letter from your employer, study institution, or confirming general work
  • Registration documents, a Company Registration Certificate, or a Tax Clearance Certificate
  • An affidavit stating the reason for your application
  • Two ID-sized photographs
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement), not older than 3 months — if the utility bill is not in your name, attach an affidavit from the account holder confirming you live there, plus a copy of their ID; if you live in an informal settlement, bring an officially date-stamped letter from your ward councillor confirming your address instead
  • If the company operates from a home address: a letterhead stating the property owner's details, along with their ID copy and proof of address
  • Signed affidavit, completed in person at the licensing department

Application for a body of persons

What to bring
  • Company or organisation letterhead applying for the TRN, naming the appointed proxy and representative (include a proxy for each branch or office, if more than one)
  • Acceptable identification for the appointed representative(s) or proxy/proxies
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement) — if the utility bill is not in your name, attach an affidavit from the account holder confirming you live there, plus a copy of their ID; if you live in an informal settlement, bring an officially date-stamped letter from your ward councillor confirming your address instead
  • If the organisation operates from a home address: a letterhead stating the property owner's details, along with their ID copy and proof of address
  • Signed affidavit, completed in person at the licensing department

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is a Traffic Register Number (TRN)?

It works like an ID document for an entity that isn't a legal person, such as a trust. You'd need one to register a vehicle under that kind of entity.

Can RenewGP apply for a TRN for me?

No. We can help you gather the documents as a courtesy, but the application has to be completed by you directly with the licensing department.

Who can apply?

Both individuals (natural persons) and bodies of persons such as companies and organisations. The documents differ, so see the two checklists above.

Which form do I need?

The ANR form: Application and Notice of Traffic Register Number, available on our forms page.
