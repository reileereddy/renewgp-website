Before you go

Documents you will need

For an individual

What to bring
  • Copy of your RC1 / eNaTIS document, if you have one
  • Copy of the owner's ID (original ID card or green ID book)
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement) — if the utility bill is not in your name, attach an affidavit from the account holder confirming you live there, plus a copy of their ID; if you live in an informal settlement, bring an officially date-stamped letter from your ward councillor confirming your address instead
  • Signed affidavit, completed in person at the licensing department

For a company

What to bring
  • Copy of your RC1 / eNaTIS document, if you have one
  • Copy of the company's BRN certificate
  • Copy of the proxy's ID
  • SARS document confirming the company's VAT number
  • A company letterhead nominating a proxy and representative, signed and agreed by both
  • Proof of company address (utility bill or bank statement) — if the utility bill is not in your name, attach an affidavit from the account holder confirming you live there, plus a copy of their ID; if you live in an informal settlement, bring an officially date-stamped letter from your ward councillor confirming your address instead
  • If the company operates from a home address: a letterhead stating the property owner's details, along with their ID copy and proof of address
  • Signed affidavit, completed in person at the licensing department

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can someone else apply for my duplicate Natis (RC1)?

No. A duplicate Natis (RC1) can only be requested by the vehicle's owner, in person, at the licensing department. We can help you prepare, but we can't submit it for you.

What documents do I need?

For an individual: a copy of the RC1/eNaTIS document (if you have it), a copy of the owner's ID, proof of address, a signed affidavit and the DRC form. For a company: the BRN certificate, the proxy's ID, a SARS document confirming the VAT number, a company letterhead nominating a proxy and representative, proof of company address, a signed affidavit and the DRC form.

Which form do I use?

The DRC form: Application for Duplicate Registration/Deregistration Certificate. You can download it from our forms page. Duplicates of licence discs are not permitted.

Does RenewGP charge for this?

No — we help you get your documents together as a courtesy.
